How to Use Achievements

Recreate your current progress or experiment with new builds.

1

Recreate your current progress

Start in Unlimited mode. Mark the achievements you already own as unlocked and select their current levels.

2

Enter your UR Tokens

Enter the number of UR Tokens you currently have available.

3

Start a Budget Build

Switch to Budget Build. Your current achievement setup becomes the starting point and your entered UR Tokens become your available budget.

4

Experiment with your build

Upgrade achievements as you like. Levels you cannot afford are disabled automatically. Downgrading an achievement returns its UR Tokens to your available budget.

5

Prerequisite Achievements

Some achievements require another achievement at Level 6 and specific Palmon. Since Palmon requirements are not tracked yet, use Mark as Unlocked once you have actually unlocked the achievement ingame.

Tip

Switching from Unlimited to Budget Build uses your current achievement setup as the new starting point.
